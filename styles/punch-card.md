# Punch card

The 1928 IBM 80-column punch card: a hole is 1, no hole is 0. For series that trace computing from its earliest form to today.

## Visual

- Colors: card #E9DFC4 (background), ink #2A2723 (text, lines), print gray #8A7F68 (secondary text, texture), cobalt #1C4FB8 (emphasis), highlight #C8D5F0 (emphasis fill)
- Fonts: titles and labels in Songti SC (fallback Noto Serif CJK SC); numbers and annotations in a typewriter face (Courier New)
- Aspect ratios: video 16:9; social cover 3:4
- Texture: faint printed column digits (0–9) behind the content, a few dark rectangular punches; cover has a cut top-left corner.

## Emphasis

One point of emphasis per visual. Cobalt alone is too weak on the card color, so emphasis always combines: cobalt text or line, bold weight, a thicker stroke, and the highlight fill for cells or boxes. Everything else is ink on card.

## Diagrams

Thin ink lines, no color fills except the header tint (#DCCFAE) and the emphasis fill. Data sources in small print-gray text at the bottom.

## Illustration

Vintage technical manual engraving, fine ink linework on buff card stock, one element in cobalt blue (#1C4FB8), no text.

## Risks

Strongly nostalgic. Contemporary topics need the content to connect old and new.
