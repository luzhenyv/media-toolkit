# Warm flat

The original style: white background, two state colors, flat and friendly. For light short videos and visuals that contrast two kinds of things.

## Visual

- Colors: white #FFFFFF (background), text #222222, secondary #888888, orange-red #E4572E and blue #2F6DB5 (two categories), light fills #FDE8E1 / #E6F0FB
- Fonts: PingFang SC throughout
- Aspect ratios: video 16:9; social cover 3:4

## Emphasis

Color marks categories (orange-red vs blue), not importance. Emphasize with bold weight and size.

## Diagrams

Simple, generous whitespace; each category in its color with a light fill. Data sources in small gray text at the bottom.

## Illustration

Flat vector illustration, warm palette (cream background, orange-red #E4572E accents, muted blue), clean lines, minimal shading, friendly exaggerated expressions, no text.

## Risks

Two accent colors compete, so the key point stands out less than in single-accent styles. Less distinctive.
