# Ink and seal

A Chinese book page or manuscript: generous whitespace, black ink, and now and then a single red seal. Quiet, for history and philosophy.

## Visual

- Colors: paper #F4F4F1 (background), ink #1A1A1A (text, lines), light ink #9A9A96 (secondary), wash #E6E6E1 (fills), seal red #A8322D (emphasis)
- Fonts: Songti SC throughout (fallback Noto Serif CJK SC); titles large with slightly loose tracking; vertical titles on covers
- Aspect ratios: video 16:9; social cover 3:4
- Signature: a square red seal with the character 震, bottom corner of covers and occasionally visuals.

## Emphasis

One point of emphasis per visual, in seal red. Use it for one conclusion or the seal, never both on the same visual. Everything else is ink and gray wash.

## Diagrams

Black and white; layers separated by light-ink gray. Serif numbers.

## Illustration

Minimal Chinese ink wash illustration, black ink and soft gray washes on off-white paper, a single small red seal, generous empty space, no text.

## Risks

Serif type looks loose in dense technical charts. Keep red rare in animations.
