# Signal and noise

The brand name drawn as a rule: gray is noise, the known, the old; only what is truly new or worth seeing is blue.

## Visual

- Colors: white #FFFFFF (background), text #202124, noise gray #A3A6AD (lines, secondary, texture), light fills #F1F2F4 / #EEF0FB, signal blue #2740D8 (emphasis)
- Fonts: PingFang SC throughout; titles bold and tight; numbers regular, emphasis bold
- Aspect ratios: video 16:9; social cover 3:4
- Texture: fine scattered gray dots as background noise. Cover motif: a blue line that starts as noise and settles into a straight line.

## Emphasis

Exactly one colored element per visual: the answer to "what is actually new here?". Everything else stays gray, including lines and borders.

## Diagrams

All gray; the signal element in blue with a thicker stroke and bold numbers.

## Illustration

Minimal flat illustration in grays on white, one element in ultramarine blue (#2740D8) carries the point, subtle gray dot noise texture, no text.

## Risks

Restrained to the point of cold. Each visual must decide what its signal is.
