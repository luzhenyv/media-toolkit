# Blueprint

An engineer's blueprint: every visual reads like a drawing of a machine. Cool, precise, and steady on video.

## Visual

- Colors: blueprint #1E3553 (background), white #EEF3F8 (lines, text), grid #8FA7C0 (grid, secondary text), fill #274266 (boxes), annotation yellow #F2C14E (emphasis)
- Fonts: PingFang SC throughout; large titles in a light weight
- Aspect ratios: video 16:9; social cover 3:4
- Texture: a 40 px grid with a heavier line every 200 px. Covers carry a drawing title block in the bottom-right corner.

## Emphasis

One point of emphasis per visual: the annotation (conclusion or key number) in yellow, with a thicker stroke. Everything else is white linework.

## Diagrams

White line drawings over the grid, like engineering drawings. Dimension lines and callouts are welcome.

## Illustration

Cyanotype blueprint style technical drawing, white lines on prussian blue, one small yellow annotation, no text.

## Risks

Engineering-heavy: literature and philosophy fit less naturally. A dark background feels heavy inside light article pages.
